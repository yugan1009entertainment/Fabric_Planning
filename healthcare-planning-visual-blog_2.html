04 · Build sequence

Five steps to a driver-based plan.

01 · FILTER

Filter to actuals

Scenario = Actual and inpatient units only, so budget rows do not mix into history.

02 · FORECAST

Create the forecast measure

Sep 2026–Dec 2027, linked to actuals for closed months and open for future periods.

03 · PREDICT

Run MSTL

Trend Decomposition with MSTL, trained on Jan 2024–Aug 2026 with 12-month seasonality.

04 · FORMULAS

Add staffing formulas

Nursing hours, paid FTEs and labor cost calculate month by month.

05 · SIMULATE

Test scenarios

Model +10% and −5% volume, then compare side by side.

05 · Forecasting

Let 32 months of history teach the plan.

Monthly history runs from January 2024 through August 2026. The forecast covers September 2026 through December 2027.

Patient-day trajectory
The forecast measure creates the future months first. Then Predict fills the open periods—while actual history stays untouched.

Without filtering to Actual first, budget rows can cause 2026 to double count and teach Predict a false jump.

06 · Planning mechanics

Four constants turn hours into people and dollars.

0.885Worked share · 11.5% of paid time goes to PTO, holidays and sick days.
173.33Hours per FTE · 2,080 paid hours ÷ 12.
$41.91Blended 2027 hourly wage across RNs, LPNs and nursing assistants.
1.285Benefits load · adds 28.5% for health, retirement and payroll taxes.
Patient days × HPPD = nursing hours → ÷ hours per nurse = FTEs → × wage and benefits = cost.
