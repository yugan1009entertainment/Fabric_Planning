01 · The problem

“Volume was up” explains
only half the overrun.

Med-Surg 3 West ran over its direct-care labor budget in Q2 2026 (April to June). The usual explanation was more patients. That was true, and it covered 55% of the gap.

Med-Surg 3 West · Q2 2026
$51,295

over the direct-care labor budget

Higher census explained 55% of the overrun. Almost half came from causes nobody was discussing: nurses spending more hours per patient than the target, and a higher hourly rate driven by agency staff and overtime.

Each of those causes has a different owner. A single number on a variance report hides all three.

02 · The shift

Forecast the patients.
Let everything else follow.

Instead of typing a labor budget into a spreadsheet, forecast the one thing that drives it. Each step after that is a simple rule that one team owns.

STEP 1
Patient days
predicted from 32 months of history
Owner: strategy
STEP 2
Nursing hours
× hours each patient day needs (HPPD)
Owner: nursing
STEP 3
Paid FTEs
÷ paid hours per nurse
Owner: HR
STEP 4
Labor cost
× wage rate and benefits
Owner: finance

Change the volume forecast and every downstream number recalculates for every unit. Nobody has to re-key a staffing plan or rebuild a cost model by hand.

Four constants turn hours into people and dollars

0.885

Worked share

11.5% of paid time goes to PTO, holidays and sick days.

173.33

Hours per FTE

Paid hours in one month for one full-time person: 2,080 ÷ 12.

$41.91

Blended wage

Average 2027 hourly rate across RNs, LPNs and nursing assistants.

1.285

Benefits load

Adds 28.5% for health, retirement and payroll taxes.

patient days × HPPD = nursing hours → ÷ hours per nurse = FTEs → × wage and benefits = cost
03 · The build

Five steps to a
driver-based plan.

Here is how the plan comes together in Planning in Fabric IQ, from filtering history to comparing scenarios side by side.

1

Filter to actuals

Scenario = Actual and inpatient units only, so budget rows never mix into history.

2

Create the forecast measure

Model › Forecast: the container for Sep 2026 – Dec 2027, linked to actuals for past months.

3

Run Predict

MSTL trend decomposition with 12-month seasonality, trained on Jan 2024 – Aug 2026.

4

Add staffing formulas

Nursing hours, paid FTEs and labor cost, calculated month by month.

5

Simulate scenarios

Model › Scenario: +10% and −5% volume, then compare side by side.
